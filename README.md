# Kams Hair & Beauty — concept site (rework v2)

A full rebuild (Oct 2026) in the salon's own identity: black, electric blue
and yellow, monumental Syne posters. Their own tagline is the hero —
"Your Hair is 90% of your selfie. Take care of it."

- **The Glass Pass** — a 50-frame scroll-scrubbed flat-iron sequence: your
  scroll seals the nanoplastia in, frizz to glass.
- **The Chair Index** — their real services (Fresha/Google/promos), grouped:
  Colour · Smoothing · The Chair · + Also. No invented prices — Kams
  publishes none; the hooks are their real first-visit offers.
- **The Wall** — before/after frames from their own treatment reel, then
  verbatim Google reviews under the pattern they all share: "He took his time."
- **Book** — Kamal's own promo poster, Fresha booking + call blocks.
- Photos are Kams's real ones (their Yelp uploads + stills cropped from
  their Instagram reels). Rating shown: 5.0★ / 800 Google reviews
  (Birdeye, Oct 24 2026). Hours print Google's 10 AM–9 PM daily —
  sources conflict; confirm with Kamal before launch.
